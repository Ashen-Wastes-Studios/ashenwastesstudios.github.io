Research Areas

Creative Tools

Approach

Embedded, not external.

Most AI research treats the model as the product. We treat it as a component — one that has to coexist with rendering, physics, audio, and input at 60 frames per second. That constraint shapes everything we build.

Our systems run locally on player hardware. No cloud dependency, no latency spikes, no per-token cost. Just intelligence that lives inside the game.
