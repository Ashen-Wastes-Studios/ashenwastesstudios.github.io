Donor Assistance Program.

Direct, personalized support for your projects — available to our monthly donors.

What is the Donor Assistance Program?

The Donor Assistance Program gives you direct access to our team for hands-on help with your projects. Whether you need custom game engine technology, AI solutions, or specialized coding work, we provide one-on-one guidance and development support.

Anyone who donates $10 or more per month qualifies for this program. No matter the platform — Patreon, Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors, or direct payment — if you're supporting us at $10+/month, you're in.

What we can help with

Custom Game Engine Tech

Custom rendering pipelines, physics systems, editor tools, shader development, or help integrating Wasteland Engine into your workflow.

Custom AI Solutions

NPC behavior systems, procedural content generation, fine-tuning models for your use case, or integrating AI into your game or application.

Custom Streaming Tools

Custom OBS plugins, chat integrations, stream overlays, automation tools, or anything else you need for your streaming setup.

Custom Creator Tools

Asset pipelines, automation scripts, content management systems, or any custom software to make your creative workflow easier.

Code Review & Architecture

Get expert feedback on your codebase, help designing system architecture, or guidance on solving complex technical problems.

Priority Bug Fixes

If you're using our open source projects, get priority attention on bugs, feature requests, and pull request reviews.

How it works

  • Donate $10+/month through any platform — Patreon, Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors, PayPal, or crypto.
  • Join our Discord and open a ticket in the #donor-assistance channel with your proof of donation.
  • Tell us what you need — we'll scope out the work and give you a timeline.
  • We get to work — direct communication with our developers until your project is done.

What qualifies?

Any recurring monthly donation of $10 or more. This includes:

  • Patreon ($10+/month tier)
  • Ko-fi (monthly membership or recurring tip)
  • GitHub Sponsors ($10+/month)
  • PayPal (recurring monthly payment)
  • Crypto (equivalent monthly value)

One-time donations don't qualify, but if you set up any recurring payment of $10+/month, you're in for as long as the donation continues.

Ready to get started?

Donate $10+/month and join our Discord to unlock direct assistance from our team. We'll help you build what you need.
